OpenTune 1.1.0 is a large update: a Home that is built from what you play, Automix, bit-perfect output, a separate app for TVs, and a long list of redesigns and fixes.

**Home**
- Home's rows are ordered by how often you play from each, with a little shuffle each day. Mix rows no longer come one after another.
- Your daylist, a mix that changes through the day, shown as a large card over its own blurred covers.
- Mixes by genre, and a Discover mix for each of your top genres. They are kept for 12 hours instead of changing every time Home opens.
- Listening streaks and badges, and a first-run artist picker.
- Cards that YouTube Music repeats across shelves are shown once.
- Mixes can download by themselves on Wi-Fi while the phone charges.

**Playback**
- Automix: songs blend into each other, matched in key and tempo. It waits for the vocals to end and skips silent endings. Choose how much work it does under Settings.
- Crossfade without the gap or the repeated bit.
- Autoplay keeps the queue filled, so it never runs dry with Next greyed out.
- Bit-perfect output through OpenTune's own USB DAC driver, exclusive access to the built-in DAC, and the headphone jack, multichannel as well as stereo.
- Every song shows its bitrate, measured from the audio. Dolby Atmos (E-AC-3 and AC-4) is recognised.
- A performance profile with a battery saver.

**New features**
- OpenTune for TV is now its own app (`OpenTune-TV-v1.1.0.apk`), with sign-in from your phone. Your phone can also act as its remote, with a trackpad and buttons.
- Comments on the playing song, from YouTube.
- Smart playlists that fill themselves from rules.
- Free audiobooks from LibriVox, with chapters and your place kept.
- Pick several songs at once to like, queue, download or add to a playlist.
- Add songs to several playlists at once. Untick a playlist to take the song out of it.
- Liked songs sync with YouTube Music both ways, and YouTube Music listens come into your history.
- Discord shows what you're playing.
- An App language setting. Translations are still being filled in.

**Design**
- Redesigned Add to playlist, Fonts & readability, Account & integrations, Audio sources, Followed artists and the artist signature card.
- Playlist and artist lists open tall and pull up to full page. Comments, Followed artists and Add to playlist go edge to edge.
- Player backgrounds in the cover's own colours, full-screen playlist covers, and a smoother player opening and fold.
- The player's three-dot menu has one icon per row, and Comments is always there.
- Saved playlists can be removed from your library.

The desktop app and the web player carry this release's shared changes. The web player also has a Back button and a title bar on album and playlist pages, and covers on the mood and genre tiles.

Install over the official app to keep your settings. Choose **arm64-v8a** for most recent phones or **universal** if unsure. The release uses the official signing identity; `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains APK checksums.
