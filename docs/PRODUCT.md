# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who listen to a lot of music on Android and want the YouTube Music catalogue without ads, sign-in or a subscription. Many arrive from GitHub, Reddit, Telegram or a friend's link on a phone, often in India, and decide within a minute whether to install an APK from outside the Play Store. A smaller group comes for the TV app, the desktop app, the browser player or the early iPhone build. The project owner also uses the site's private usage dashboard to follow installs and plays.

## Product Purpose

The website explains what OpenTune is, shows it working, and gets the right build onto the visitor's device with as little doubt as possible. Success is a visitor who understands the app in a few seconds, trusts the download (open source, release notes, clear install steps), and installs it. The web player at /player/ lets people listen in the browser straight away.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, generous. The app is the star; the site stays quiet around it, the way a good audio product page lets the sound speak. Confident without shouting, plain words, no marketing fog. The coral brand colour appears with intent, not everywhere.

## Anti-references

- Busy feature showcases with many interactive toy demos competing for attention.
- Startup SaaS landing pages: hero metric blocks, identical icon card grids, gradient text, glass cards everywhere.
- Piracy-site aesthetics: download buttons shouting, ads, countdowns, dark-pattern prompts.
- Long empty stretches that only fill in after scroll animations.

## Design Principles

1. Show the app, not adjectives: real screenshots and short captions over claims.
2. One clear next step per section, and the download always within reach.
3. Earn trust plainly: open source, release notes, what is and isn't collected, how to install.
4. Quiet surface, warm accent: dark, calm space with the coral reserved for actions and moments.
5. Fast on a mid-range phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus, full keyboard use, real text (not text in images). Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative and content never depends on an animation finishing. Layouts work from 320px wide without horizontal scroll.
